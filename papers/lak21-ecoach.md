# Does tailored coaching help in gateway courses?

Students who engaged more with ECoach, a system that tailors advice to each student, earned modestly better
grades in large gateway courses, beyond what their prior record predicted.

## Question

Is engagement with ECoach associated with better outcomes in large gateway courses?

## Data

Five courses, 3,599 students (from the abstract).

## Approach

- Observational study; ECoach recommends resources based on each student's profile, performance and use
- Clustering to group students by engagement; regression linking engagement to outcomes

## Findings

From the abstract only; the paper is paywalled.

- Engagement had statistically significant, small positive associations with final grade and with grade anomaly
  (grade relative to the student's record elsewhere)
- The courses with the strongest link offered a little extra credit for use, and the gains went well beyond what
  that incentive alone would explain

## Why it matters

Tailored technology is a promising way to tackle low grades, competitive climates and weak belonging in gateway
courses.
