# What faculty need to use learning-analytics data

Interviews with faculty found that those already involved in teaching reform used institutional learning-analytics
data, and that unfamiliarity with quantitative education data was the main barrier for everyone else.

## Question

What leads faculty to seek out the educational data their university collects, what do they want from it, what
gets in the way, and how could their use of it be better supported?

## Data

Interviews with faculty at one institution (the abstract says 14).

## Approach

Qualitative interviews about faculty use of university data such as placement exams, registrations and
demographics.

## Findings

From the abstract only; the full text wasn't reachable.

- Most interviewees were already involved in education reform and used the data for that work
- The main barriers were unfamiliarity with quantitative education data and limited awareness of what learning
  analytics can offer

## Why it matters

The data only help if they're analyzed and applied well; the authors recommend targeted outreach and better use
of existing resources.
