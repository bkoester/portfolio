# Transcript of the Future, released as research data

The Transcript of the Future measures, computed for the full undergraduate population of 19 public universities and
released through ICPSR as a restricted research dataset that other teams can build on.

## Question

What ready-made measures of the undergraduate experience can be built from transcript data alone, for researchers
studying how college connects to long-term outcomes?

## Data

- College and Beyond II, commissioned by the Mellon Foundation: bachelor's-seeking undergraduates at 19 public
  institutions in seven systems, about 2000 to 2021 [User Guide, 1.1]
- The underlying administrative data: 1,311,818 students, 9,171,187 student-terms and 46,863,737 course enrollments
  [User Guide, 2.3.1]

## Approach

- Two sets of measures derived only from administrative records: Transcript of the Future and Course Diversity
  [study page]
- Linked to the series' other studies (administrative records, alumni survey, course content) by person and course
  identifiers [User Guide, 1.3]

## Findings

- Transcript of the Future: seven files, 10 variables each; four of them credited to the LAK17 paper (student
  effect, course effect, breadth, major diversity) [study page]
- Course Diversity: seven files, 19 variables each [study page]
- Restricted use: approved researchers only, through ICPSR's Virtual Data Enclave under a data use agreement
  [User Guide, 1.5]

## Why it matters

A published, documented dataset that turns a research method into infrastructure other researchers use.
