# Public lens models for 37 clusters

Hubble images and spectroscopy of 37 clusters found in Sloan data were used to map where each cluster's mass
sits, and the maps and images were released for anyone studying the magnified galaxies behind them.

## Question

Using HST imaging and ground-based spectroscopy, what strong-lensing mass models can be built for 37 SDSS Giant
Arcs Survey clusters, and how reliable is each?

## Data

- 37 SGAS clusters selected from SDSS DR7 imaging [Abstract; Sec. 2]
- HST WFC3 imaging from GO-13003, 107 orbits in Cycle 20, four filters per cluster [Sec. 3.1; Table 1]
- Spectroscopy from Gemini/GMOS, Magellan (IMACS, LDSS3, FIRE), APO/DIS and MMT [Sec. 3.2]
- Cluster redshifts from 0.17637 to 0.656 [Table 3]

## Approach

- Identified multiple images of background galaxies and confirmed redshifts where possible [Sec. 3.2; Table 4]
- Modeled each cluster with Lenstool as cluster-scale plus galaxy-scale mass halos, sampled by MCMC [Sec. 4]
- Graded each model A (robust), B (acceptable) or C (under-constrained) [Sec. 4.3]

## Findings

- Most lensed background galaxies in the sample lie at 1 < z < 3 [Sec. 1]
- Modeled sources reach z = 5.2, a Lyman-alpha emitter behind SDSS J0915+3826 [Sec. 4.3]
- Velocity dispersions range from 556 ± 178 km/s (SDSS J0004−0103, group-like) to 1597 ± 380 km/s (SDSS J1138+2754) [Table 3]
- Quality grades range from A (e.g. SDSS J0146−0929) to C- (SDSS J1002+2031) [Sec. 4.3]
- Reduced HST images and lens model outputs are released as high-level data products [Abstract]

## Why it matters

The authors frame the models as a resource for studying highly magnified background galaxies and for reliably
identifying their multiple images.
