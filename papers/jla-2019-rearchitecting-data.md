# Turning administrative records into research data

How the University of Michigan built a shared, documented research dataset from its administrative records so
learning-analytics researchers could stop rebuilding the data themselves, and what other institutions can copy.

## Question

How can an institution make its administrative student data usable for learning-analytics research, and keep it
useful as the research community grows?

## Data

University of Michigan administrative data: academic records, demographics and related outcomes. The abstract gives
no counts.

## Approach

- Case description of the situation before the dataset existed and why it was built
- How the dataset was designed, built and maintained as a collaborative, extensible resource
- Researcher access, documentation, and upkeep for a growing user base

## Findings

From the abstract only; the PDF wasn't reachable.

- Delivered a jointly developed, extensible research dataset drawn from administrative records
- Ends with recommendations for institutions that want to replicate the effort

## Why it matters

A model other institutions can follow to make their own administrative data research-ready.
