# Equity-analytics workshop (LASI 2019)

Taught "Probing Equity with Data" at the 2019 Learning Analytics Summer Institute, with R code and synthetic data
participants could run on their own laptops.

## Approach

Course-level diversity, equity and inclusion statistics: demographic diversity, grade penalties by group, and
persistence into the next course; LASSO for dimension reduction ahead of propensity-score matching
(`optmatch`, `lars`); census income joined by ZIP code (`censusapi`).

## Related papers

- McKay, Grom & Koester. Categorization, Intersectionality, and Learning Analytics. 2018 (Deep Blue)
