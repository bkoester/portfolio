# WCC pathfinder

What does it actually cost, and which courses actually count, to start at Washtenaw Community College and finish
at a Michigan university?

## The problem

Community-college transfer saves money only if the credits transfer. Students and families can't easily see which
course and major combinations carry over, or what a 2+2 path costs against four years at the university.

## Data

Public course catalogs and transfer equivalencies for WCC and five universities: U-M Ann Arbor, Eastern Michigan,
Michigan State, Central Michigan and Grand Valley State. Designed to add more universities later.

## Approach

Course and major mapping, transfer matching, and cost models for tuition with living at home. Runs on the
[EC2 host](ec2-platform.md).

## Results

Open question: one worked example path and what it saves.
